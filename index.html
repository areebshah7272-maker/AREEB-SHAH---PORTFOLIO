Case study — SADAT STORE

Commerce / Product design / Development

A storefront designed as a complete product system.

The brief was simple: make online shopping feel considered without slowing people down. The answer was a modular commerce experience with a strong editorial layer and clear paths from first browse to repeat purchase.

Role
Product design & development
Focus
Discovery, trust & conversion
Scope
Customer and admin journeys
01

Challenge

Premium, not precious.

Build a distinct brand experience while keeping product finding and purchasing direct.

02

Direction

Editorial clarity.

Use confident type, restrained color and generous space to make the catalogue feel curated.

03

System

One connected flow.

Treat storefront, cart, checkout, account and admin as parts of the same product language.

Customer journey

Every step knows
what comes next.

Clear wayfinding and consistent interaction patterns reduce effort across the full buying journey.

  1. 01DiscoverCampaigns & collections
  2. 02BrowseFilters & product detail
  3. 03DecideTrust & product context
  4. 04CheckoutFocused purchase flow
  5. 05ReturnAccount & order history
Designed to evolve

The modular foundation supports future inventory, payment, fulfilment and analytics integrations without redesigning the customer experience.
